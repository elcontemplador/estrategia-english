Editor and contributor

Pablo Martín Diez

Pablo Martín Diez is the editor of estrategIA and Academic Director of ALEPH Educational Institution. His professional work spans political communication, leadership, electoral campaigns and crisis management. He has led training and consultancy projects for political institutions and universities in Europe, the Americas and Africa, and has taught at the Universidad Internacional de La Rioja and universities in Latin America.

His work also addresses the use of generative AI in politics and the challenges it poses for education and organisations. His contributions to estrategIA include reflections on democratic ethics, the changing role of teachers and the social contract in an age of AI. He combines these subjects with a background in history, journalism and political communication.
