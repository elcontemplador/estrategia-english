Editorial review and proofreading

Sofía García Morales

Sofía García Morales is responsible for editorial review and proofreading at estrategIA. She is also responsible for online communication at ALEPH Educational Institution, where she works as a consultant. Alongside her editorial work, she contributes reporting on the practical questions that public institutions face as they begin to use artificial intelligence.

Her account of MUNICIP-IA, a public-sector AI event in Galapagar, examines municipal services, institutional readiness and the political implications of technological change. The report explores how councils can set priorities, develop internal capabilities and apply AI with an understanding of its limits. It also considers digital sovereignty and the wider challenges that changes in work and education pose for public decision-making.
