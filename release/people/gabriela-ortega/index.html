Gabriela Ortega Jarrín

Gabriela Ortega Jarrín is Strategy Director at ALEPH Educational Institution and a contributor to estrategIA. A political consultant, she has worked with electoral campaigns and public institutions in Spain and Latin America. She holds a doctorate in Public Communication from the Universidad Complutense de Madrid, and her research examines intangible assets and brand management in the public sector.

Her professional experience combines consultancy with university teaching and publications on political and institutional communication. For estrategIA, she has written about developments from OpenAI and Google and reported from Google Cloud Summit Madrid 2025. Her summit account examines public services, digital sovereignty, skills and regulation, placing the technology presented at the event in the context of public-sector decisions.
