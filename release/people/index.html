The people behind estrategIA

Fernando Nieto Lobato founded and directs estrategIA and is its principal writer. Pablo Martín Diez and Sofía García Morales work alongside him on editing and editorial review.

Explore the guest authors ↓

Editorial team

Founder, director and lead writer

Fernando Nieto Lobato

Founder, director and lead writer of estrategIA. A strategic consultant based in Salamanca, Fernando leads marketing and digital innovation at ALEPH and writes about AI, politics, government and public communication.

Profile and essays →

Editor and contributor

Pablo Martín Diez

Editor of estrategIA and Academic Director of ALEPH. Pablo works on political communication, leadership and training, and contributes essays on AI, education, democratic ethics and organisational change.

Profile and essays →

Editorial review and proofreading

Sofía García Morales

Sofía handles editorial review and proofreading at estrategIA and online communication at ALEPH. A consultant and contributor, she has reported on municipal AI adoption and the institutional judgement it requires.
