Five good AI tools with Black Friday discounts

The November 2024 archive of estrategIA's Black Friday selection: Pictory, Kling, Mimic PC, Freepik and Poe, with the original offers and disclosures preserved.

Fernando Nieto LobatoAuthor
27 November 2024Originally published in Spanish
10 min readEnglish edition · 29 September 2026

Archive note: this article was published on 27 November 2024. All recommendations, prices, discounts, availability and deadlines below belong to that historical Black Friday selection; they are not current offers or purchasing advice.

Once again, we have reached the shopping and sales season par excellence: Black Friday. This week, we have prepared a special article recommending a wide variety of AI tools with particularly substantial discounts at this time of year. Unfortunately, many of the most interesting services — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Midjourney, HeyGen, Gamma, Perplexity and others — do not offer discounts at this time. But there is also an enormous range of AI tools to choose from, and you are very likely to find something of interest in the following paragraphs.

Before we continue, two points. No tool pays us — at least for now — to review or recommend it, so every suggestion reflects our own appreciation after having used it at some point. The second consideration concerns the newsletter's schedule: this issue will be published on Wednesday 27 November, with Black Friday on the 29th and Cyber Monday, which also tends to bring special digital offers, on 2 December. Most companies have brought their discounts and deals forward, some even to the end of last week, but further interesting offers may appear after this issue of estrategIA is published.

Recommended tools with discounts¶

1. Pictory AI¶

Our first suggestion is Pictory AI. Although it lacks some of the advanced capabilities offered by tools such as HeyGen or Synthesia, it provides a wide range of options for working with video. Institución Educativa ALEPH has used it quite intensively for political training simulations, with very satisfactory results. It is now offering 50% off all annual plans.

3. Mimic PC¶

Here we have something other than a tool: relatively inexpensive virtual servers on which to run freely available AI tools with computing power that our personal machines do not normally have, along with all the advantages of privacy and control. Since some readers may want to know more about running models locally, we refer you to the video in the original newsletter's “recommendation of the week” section, which addresses that subject.

I regularly run workflows on Mimic PC, especially with Flux AI, but there are many options that are relatively straightforward to run. A word of caution, though: this gives us much more control but requires more learning than simply opening a web service and doing something. Here, you have to choose a tool, launch a server and usually create or customise a workflow.

The three visible cards contain the following descriptions.

Chatbot-Ollama — Easy; latest

Chatbot Ollama is an open source chat UI for Ollama. It is a flexible and scalable chatbot framework built with Python. Ollama Chatbot utilizes Flask, a lightweight Web services framework that provides developers with an easy-to-use API interface to receive and respond to chat requests. This design allows Ollama to seamlessly integrate into any application that supports HTTP requests, including Web and mobile applications. Extensibility To meet different needs, Ollama Chatbot supports a plug-in system. Developers can create custom plug-ins to add new features, such as integrating third-party apis, implementing specific NLP models, and more.

  1. Easy to get started: Clear documentation and examples make it easy for beginners to get started.
  2. Modularity: Components are loosely coupled for easy replacement or upgrade.
  3. Customization: Free to adjust conversation policies according to business

The last sentence ends there in the screenshot.

FaceFusion — Easy; V2.6.0; v3.0.1

FaceFusion is the next generation face swapper and enhancer to create realistic and high-quality results. The technology is based on deep learning models to learn and understand facial features, as well as efficient real-time rendering technology. It can locate and track key points of the face accurately, such as the eyes, nose and mouth, which is the foundation for seamless facial fusion.

  1. Integration with top face-enhancing algorithms.
  2. Frame enhancer that improve the entire video's quality.
  3. User-friendly interface tailored for both beginners and experts.

ComfyUI — Hard; latest; v0.3.4; v0.2.7

ComfyUI is an AI drawing tool that utilizes StableDiffusion, offering a sleek and versatile interface coupled with an efficient workflow. It supports a variety of plugins to enhance image generation efficiency and creative experience.

  1. It is more lightweight than WebUI and has a simpler, more flexible interface.
  2. A more efficient and flexible workflow, with less resource consumption and faster rendering speed.
  3. ComfyUI breaks down the workflow into rearrangeable elements, allowing you to effortlessly create your own components.

Each card also has “Get started”, “How to use?”, “Learn more” and tutorial controls. Tutorial titles are truncated in the screenshot; no missing text or destinations have been reconstructed.

4. Freepik¶

Founded in Málaga in 2010, Freepik is a leading graphics resource platform offering millions of images, vectors and PSD files. Recently, it has invested heavily in artificial intelligence; we discussed the interesting Pikaso tool in issue 10 of estrategIA. Its latest acquisition, Magnific.ai — our tool of the week in issue 16, which also has Black Friday offers — strengthens this strategy by adding technology for optimising and transforming images.

Although we do not know whether this is strictly a Black Friday offer, Poe — our recommended AI tool in issue 2 of estrategIA — has just launched a new entry-level tier. It could be highly interesting for anyone wanting access to today's best AI models, for both text and image generation, without using them intensively. This is what Poe said about the offer in an email received this week by everyone who, at that point, had only a free account.
