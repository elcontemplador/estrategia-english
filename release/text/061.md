Once again, we have reached the shopping and sales season par excellence: Black Friday. **This week, we have prepared a special article recommending a wide variety of AI tools with particularly substantial discounts at this time of year.** Unfortunately, many of the most interesting services — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Midjourney, HeyGen, Gamma, Perplexity and others — do not offer discounts at this time. But there is also an enormous range of AI tools to choose from, and you are very likely to find something of interest in the following paragraphs.

Before we continue, two points. No tool pays us — at least for now — to review or recommend it, so every suggestion reflects our own appreciation after having used it at some point. The second consideration concerns the newsletter's schedule: this issue will be published on Wednesday 27 November, with Black Friday on the 29th and Cyber Monday, which also tends to bring special digital offers, on 2 December. Most companies have brought their discounts and deals forward, some even to the end of last week, but further interesting offers may appear after this issue of estrategIA is published.

### 3. [Mimic PC](https://www.mimicpc.com)

Here we have something other than a tool: relatively inexpensive virtual servers on which to run freely available AI tools with computing power that our personal machines do not normally have, along with all the advantages of privacy and control. Since some readers may want to know more about running models locally, we refer you to the video in the original newsletter's “recommendation of the week” section, which addresses that subject.

I regularly run workflows on Mimic PC, especially with Flux AI, but there are many options that are relatively straightforward to run. A word of caution, though: this gives us much more control but requires more learning than simply opening a web service and doing something. Here, you have to choose a tool, launch a server and usually create or customise a workflow.
