## Comparing several parties’ election manifestos using a large context window, with Google Gemini Pro 1.5

*A context window is, roughly speaking, the amount of data a large language model can work with directly.*

At its developer conference last month, [Google announced that it was opening access to Google Gemini Pro 1.5 in new countries, including those of the European Union](https://blog.google/technology/developers/gemini-gemma-developer-updates-may-2024/). This language model, one of the best on most benchmarks, although not yet at GPT-4o’s level, allows a context window of up to one million tokens, equivalent to several hundred book pages. This has allowed us to ask it for a comparative analysis of the [manifestos of different parties](https://www.europapress.es/nacional/noticia-elecciones-europeas-2024-consulta-aqui-programas-electorales-psoe-pp-vox-sumar-podemos-otras-formaciones-20240528095950.html) contesting the European elections. The initial idea was to include the PP and Vox manifestos too, but because they are much shorter and designed in a different format, we decided to focus the analysis solely on Spain’s three main left-wing parties. For this example, we asked it to work on education proposals.

We uploaded the manifestos. Those three alone add up to more than 150,000 tokens, exceeding the size GPT-4 can work with, for example: 128,000 tokens.

### Strengths and weaknesses

**Sumar — strengths:**

- A holistic approach covering early childhood through higher education.
- Strong emphasis on social justice and equal opportunities.
- Concrete proposals such as the directive to harmonise educational investment and the climate pass.

**Sumar — weaknesses:**

- A lack of detail on implementing some proposals, such as the European evaluation agency.

**PSOE — strengths:**

- Emphasis on quality, inclusion and lifelong learning.
- Commitment to digitalisation and artificial intelligence skills.
- Commitment to Erasmus+ and European Universities.

**PSOE — weaknesses:**

- Insufficient ambition in educational investment, with a target of 5% of GDP.
- Limited attention to early childhood and vocational education.

**Podemos — strengths:**

- Strong commitment to public, free, high-quality education.
- Emphasis on democratising education and citizen participation.
- Commitment to universal education from birth to age three.

**Podemos — weaknesses:**

- A lack of specific detail on financing its proposals.
- Fewer proposals than Sumar and PSOE on matters such as Erasmus+ or vocational education.

### Conclusions

The three manifestos present progressive visions of education in the European Union. However, there are notable differences in their approaches and priorities. Sumar stands out for its ambition in educational investment and social justice. PSOE prioritises quality, digitalisation and lifelong learning. Podemos focuses on defending public education and democratisation. The choice among these models will depend on European citizens’ views of education’s role in building a fairer, more equal Europe, prepared for future challenges.

It is crucial to analyse these proposals in detail and assess their feasibility to build a European education system that responds to the needs of twenty-first-century society.

*End of translated screenshot.*

[You can try Gemini Pro 1.5 for free on this website](https://aistudio.google.com/). It is a highly recommended option if you want to work with several documents simultaneously, or with very long documents. In other tests, we have obtained satisfactory retrieval of information while working with up to 800 pages.

Given that the vast majority of people do not read election manifestos, AI tools may now provide quick, visual access to summaries and comparisons, something parties themselves will have to consider when writing them. For example, if I am undecided between two or three parties and a particular issue matters a great deal to me, I could ask AI for a detailed comparative summary of that issue and have it within seconds. It is also true that there is considerable and understandable scepticism about how far manifestos are subsequently delivered. Still, we might at least ask whether systems of this kind will contribute to more rational voting.

## GPT-4o’s new data analysis system

Also last May, OpenAI released the text version of its new model, GPT-4o — we are still waiting for the rollout of the new advanced voice interface. [The model improves data analysis](https://openai.com/index/improvements-to-data-analysis-in-chatgpt/), and its ability to work with statistics and generate charts has been specifically enhanced. Unfortunately, unlike the leading women experts in electoral statistics at the ALEPH Educational Institution, my knowledge is quite limited. Yet the tool makes previously unimaginable things very easy and extraordinarily quick.

I think the following example makes this particularly clear. I uploaded [a CSV document containing turnout data for every European election](https://results.elections.europa.eu/en/tools/download-datasheets/) to its data analysis tool. I asked it to suggest different data visualisations, and in less than a minute I had it produce the following chart. Initially, it produced the same chart with very large lettering, causing the values to overlap; I simply asked it to reduce the size, and that solved the problem.

## Analysing charts to test GPT-4o’s image understanding and the capabilities of a model trained multimodally from the outset

*Multimodal: a model trained on text, audio and images, rather than text alone.*

For this test, [we used a screenshot of these European Union charts](https://results.elections.europa.eu/en/tools/comparative-tool/) and made the following initial request:

> Act as an outstanding political analyst; think step by step. Based on the information in this image, write a brief summary of how the European Parliament has evolved.

### Comparative analysis

1. *Reduction in the total number of seats: the European Parliament went from 751 seats in the 2014–2019 term to 705 in the 2019–2024 term. This reduction is partly due to the United Kingdom’s departure from the European Union, Brexit.*

2. *Fewer seats for the main groups:*

   - *EPP: the European People’s Party group fell from 221 to 177 seats, a significant loss.*
   - *S&D: the Progressive Alliance of Socialists and Democrats also saw a reduction, from 191 to 140 seats.*

3. *Reconfiguration and strengthening of centrist and green groups:*

   - *Renew Europe, previously ALDE: increased its representation from 67 to 102 seats, consolidating its position as an important force in the centre of the European political spectrum.*
   - *Greens/EFA: increased their presence from 50 to 72 seats, reflecting greater support for green and progressive policies.*

4. *Emergence of new groups:*

   - *ID, Identity and Democracy: a new group that obtained 59 seats, bringing together right-wing and Eurosceptic political forces.*
   - *The Left, European United Left/Nordic Green Left: this group saw its representation fall from 52 to 37 seats.*

5. *Relative stability among non-attached members, NI: the number of MEPs unaffiliated to any group remained relatively constant, with a slight decline from 52 to 50 seats.*

### Conclusion

*The European Parliament’s composition has changed notably between the two terms. Traditional groups such as the EPP and S&D have seen their representation decline, while centrist groups such as Renew Europe and green groups such as Greens/EFA have gained influence. The emergence of new groups such as Identity and Democracy also indicates a diversification of Europe’s political landscape, with greater representation for Eurosceptic and right-wing positions. This change in the distribution of seats reflects a more fragmented and plural European electorate, with growing concern about environmental issues and a greater presence of Eurosceptic movements.*

**We then asked it to use this knowledge to continue working with Europa Estoica Unida, the fictional party we generated at estrategIA in April to “stand” in the European Parliament elections:**

> In this landscape, what opportunities do you think would open up for a pro-European party such as Europa Estoica Unida? I will give you its principles.

We attached the [Europa Estoica Unida principles generated for us in issue 28 of estrategIA](https://open.substack.com/pub/estrategiabyaleph/p/estrategia-28-explorando-las-posibilidades?r=2tyybn&utm_campaign=post&utm_medium=web).

This is the **response** it generated:

### Analysis of opportunities for “Europa Estoica Unida” in the European Parliament

*Europa Estoica Unida, with its principles founded on wisdom, justice, self-control, fortitude and cosmopolitanism, can find significant opportunities in the European Parliament’s current landscape. The following analysis examines how these principles can align with the European Union’s current trends and needs.*

#### 1. Political and social context

*The European Parliament has experienced notable political fragmentation, with increased representation for green, centrist and Eurosceptic parties. This diversified environment offers fertile ground for new political formations able to provide a coherent and distinctive vision. In particular, the values of wisdom and justice resonate with the demands of European citizens seeking comprehensive and sustainable long-term solutions.*

#### 2. Strategic opportunities

**1. Dialogue and cooperation**

- *Strengthening alliances: Europa Estoica Unida can collaborate with groups such as Renew Europe and Greens/EFA, which share interests in sustainability and social justice. Synergies with these groups can help advance policies promoting a more equitable and sustainable Europe.*
- *Promoting dialogue: given its focus on self-control and wisdom, the party can position itself as a neutral mediator in polarised debates, offering solutions based on knowledge and rationality.*

**2. Social justice and sustainability**

- *Leading green initiatives: a commitment to sustainability can align with the Greens/EFA agenda, providing a basis for collaboration on environmental and climate policies.*
- *Equity policies: an emphasis on justice can attract voters seeking greater social and economic equality, by proposing inclusive and fair policies.*

**3. Resilience and self-control**

- *Crisis management: Europa Estoica Unida can distinguish itself in managing crises, such as the COVID-19 pandemic or the climate crisis, through proposals balancing immediate responses with long-term planning.*
- *Education and training: promote educational programmes encouraging self-control and resilience among citizens, in line with a vision of Europe acting from moral strength and rationality.*

**4. Cosmopolitanism**

- *Inclusion and diversity: promoting cosmopolitanism can attract a diverse voter base, standing out in the defence of human rights and the integration of migrants and refugees.*
- *External relations: a vision of global fraternity can position Europa Estoica Unida as a key actor in EU foreign policy, promoting international cooperation and peace.*

#### 3. Challenges and mitigation

- *Electoral competition: political fragmentation means intense competition. It is crucial for Europa Estoica Unida to define its value proposition clearly and distinguish itself from other parties.*
- *Effective communication: translating Stoic principles into concrete policies and communicating them effectively to European citizens will be fundamental to winning support.*
- *Adapting to change: flexibility and adaptability will be vital to responding to rapid changes in the political and social environment.*

*Europa Estoica Unida has an opportunity to fill a niche in today’s European Parliament, offering a vision of governance based on Stoic principles that resonate with growing demand for social justice, sustainability and peaceful cooperation. Its success will depend on its ability to form strategic alliances, communicate its values effectively and adapt to a dynamic political environment. With a well-defined strategy, it can become an influential actor in European politics, promoting a fairer, more resilient and united continent.*

**Integrating artificial intelligence into politics**, as we see every week in estrategIA, **not only improves the ability to analyse and compare election manifestos, but also helps politicians, advisers and citizens make more informed and balanced decisions.** Used well, advanced tools such as Google Gemini Pro 1.5 and GPT-4o can represent a crucial advance in democratising access to information and improving transparency and accountability in politics. In an increasingly complex and diversified electoral landscape, the ability to use these technologies effectively will be decisive for campaigns’ success and for understanding the policies proposed.
