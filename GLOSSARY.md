# Working translation glossary

Context takes priority over a mechanical substitution.

| Spanish | Preferred English | Use |
|---|---|---|
| inteligencia artificial / IA | artificial intelligence / AI | Keep estrategIA unchanged as a brand. |
| política | politics / policy | Politics for power and actors; policy for substantive decisions. |
| políticas públicas | public policy / public policies | Singular for the field; plural for distinct interventions. |
| gobierno | government | Governance only when referring to systems and processes of governing. |
| gobernanza | governance | Do not flatten into government. |
| administración pública | public administration / the public sector | Choose according to whether institution, practice or sector is meant. |
| capacidad estatal | state capacity | Distinguish from an individual employee's capabilities. |
| servidores públicos | public servants | Civil servants where the original specifically means the civil service. |
| soberanía | sovereignty | Preserve operational conditions and limits. |
| rendición de cuentas | accountability | Distinguish from transparency alone. |
| fiscalización | scrutiny / oversight | Scrutiny for public examination; oversight for institutional supervision. |
| renta básica universal | universal basic income | UBI after first definition, where needed. |
| formación | education / training | Education for broad learning; training for applied skills. |
| aprendizaje | learning | Do not claim learning outcomes where only tools are described. |
| computación | compute / computing | Compute for capacity as a resource. |
| capacidades | capabilities / capacity | Capabilities for abilities; capacity for systemic ability or resources. |
| transformación digital | digital transformation | Preserve the process and institutional dimensions. |
| brecha digital | digital divide | Digital divides when distinct gaps are specified. |
| programa | program / programme | Program for computer software, including British English; programme for an educational or public initiative. |
