# English edition: editorial method

The original Spanish essay is the source of record. This edition translates its main argument in full, with meaningful notes, links, tables and illustrations. Newsletter news round-ups and recurring subscription material are outside that article boundary.

## Translation

Use international English and British spelling. Preserve first-person voice, the strength of claims, uncertainty, counterarguments and historical context. Avoid adding conclusions or silently correcting the author's argument. Preserve proper names and provide a brief explanation of unfamiliar institutions where needed.

Keep quotations attributable. Use verified original English wording only when actually checked; otherwise identify a quotation translated from the Spanish source. Keep book and report titles identifiable. Translate meaningful image text in an adjacent labelled transcription where the original graphic is retained.

Dates, authors, article boundaries and URLs require evidence. Do not infer authors from the newsletter owner. Coauthored texts must retain every author; the current pilot template will need an authors-array extension before those entries are imported.

## Review and release

1. Establish the main-article boundary against the source.
2. Translate and record uncertainties.
3. Have a separate reviewer compare the full Spanish and English texts.
4. Record assisted review against the exact text and editorial metadata hash.
5. Obtain editorial acceptance of that exact version and resolve contributor permissions where applicable.
6. Set a persistent English publication date per essay; build and check the public release.
7. Stage only the checked public output and deploy it with the manual Pages workflow.

A change to approved text or editorial metadata invalidates its recorded hash. Technical QA and assisted review cannot supply human acceptance.

Historical factual concerns belong in the editorial review log. Do not rewrite old predictions as current reporting. Identify substantive corrections or additions to readers.

## Discovery

Serve the complete article in HTML without requiring JavaScript. Generate Markdown, catalogue, structured data, feed and sitemap from the same approved records. Retain the original Spanish source and publication date. These measures make content accessible and attributable; they do not guarantee search indexing, model training or citations.
